Home screens (390 x 844 pt, iPhone)

png/   Flat images at 3x (1170 x 2532).
html/  The screens as web pages. Open in a browser (needs internet for the font).

States
  1-home-set        Locked. Pick minutes on the wheel, then slide the red thumb to unlock.
  2-home-mid-slide  The red thumb partway along the track; the label fades as it passes.
  3-home-running    Unlocked. The wheel is hidden; slide the cream thumb to lock early.

Type
  Chivo SemiBold (600) for all text.
  Time left: 112 pt, -3% tracking, tabular figures, 0.92 line height.
  Labels: 12 pt caps, 14% tracking. Date: 13 pt. Status line: 11 pt.
  Wheel numbers: 50 pt, -3% tracking, tabular figures. "MIN" label: 12 pt caps.

Colour
  Ink           #1D1A16
  Secondary     #7A7163
  Red           #D2402A   (live status dot)
  Unlocked text #B8361F
  Cream         #F2ECE1 to #D9CFBE background, #FFFEFB to #E2D9C9 plastic

Scroll wheel (342 x 300 pt)
  Selected row: 60 pt band, 14 pt radius, #3B2F1E at 7% with a soft inner shadow.
  Rows step about 54 pt from the center and shrink vertically and fade with distance
  (100%, 50%, 37%, 24% opacity); the top and bottom edges fade out.

Slider (342 x 66 pt, 12 pt above the bottom margin)
  Track: 18 pt radius, #CABFA9 to #E2D9CA, inset shadows (a recessed groove).
  Thumb: 86 x 54 pt, 13 pt radius, 6 pt inset, with a 3 pt lip and a soft shadow.
    Unlock: red #F06A4D to #C9391F, lip #97291A, chevrons on top.
    Lock:   cream #FFFEFA to #E3DACB, lip #BFB39E.
  Label: 12 pt caps, 14% tracking, rgba(84,70,50,0.62) with a 1 pt white text shadow.
    "SLIDE TO UNLOCK 15 MIN" (minutes follow the wheel) or "SLIDE TO LOCK".
